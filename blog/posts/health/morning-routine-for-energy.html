My Morning Routine for All-Day Energy

I used to wake up groggy and reach for coffee immediately. Here's what I changed.

The routine

1. Drink a glass of water before anything else
2. Get sunlight within 30 minutes of waking
3. Move for 10 minutes — stretching, walking, anything
4. Eat a protein-rich breakfast

Why it works

Morning light resets your body clock. Hydration and movement wake up your metabolism. And protein keeps energy stable instead of spiking and crashing.

It took about two weeks to feel the difference. Small consistent habits beat big occasional efforts.
