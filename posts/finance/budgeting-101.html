Budgeting 101: Where to Start

Money management doesn't have to be complicated. Here's a simple way to start.

The 50/30/20 rule

A popular starting point is dividing your income into three buckets:

50% for needs — rent, groceries, bills
30% for wants — dining out, entertainment
20% for savings and debt repayment

Track for one month

Before changing anything, track every expense for 30 days. Most people are surprised by where money actually goes.

Automate savings

Set up an automatic transfer on payday. You'll never miss money you don't see.

Start small. Even 5% builds the habit, and habits matter more than amounts.
