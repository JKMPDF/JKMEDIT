Ayurveda Basics: Understanding the Three Doshas

Ayurveda, the ancient Indian system of medicine, teaches that health depends on balancing three energies called doshas.

Vata — air and space

Light, dry, and mobile. Vata types are creative and quick-thinking but may struggle with anxiety or irregular digestion.

Pitta — fire and water

Intense and driven. Pitta types are ambitious and focused, but can run hot — literally — with inflammation and irritability.

Kapha — earth and water

Calm and steady. Kapha types are loyal and strong, but may need extra motivation to stay active.

Most people are a mix of two doshas. Understanding yours is the first step to eating and living in balance.
